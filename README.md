#**Harry Kane Dashboard** 

This is a simple passion project of mine as I am in awe of how phenomenal Harry Kane is as a striker.

This project is aimed at identifying his contribution towards goals based on the leagues he has or is currently playing in. The timeline starts from the 2013/14 season to the end of the 2025/2026 season.

The major stats used includes the matches, goals scored, assists as well as goals and assists over his career. The three graphs in the report includes:

•	A line chart for total shots per year

•	A bar chart with Kanes’ best five goalscoring seasons in the leagues he has played in

•	A column chart with all goals he has scored from the 2013/14 season to last season, incorporated with a Tooltip that appears when hovering over the chart that lists the games played, total goals and assists as well as the team he played for.

Based on calculations provided by Power BI over the course of his career, We can assume that this season, he is projected to score at least 23 goals and assist five times, contributing to an overall of 29 goal contributions for Bayern Munich in the Bundesliga this season
